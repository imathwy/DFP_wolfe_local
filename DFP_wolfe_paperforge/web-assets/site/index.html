A counterexample to global convergence of classical DFP under the standard strong Wolfe conditions

Benqi Liu, Zichen Wang, Zaiwen Wen, Liwei Zhang, and Yaxiang Yuan

A globally uniformly convex C2 objective admits a classical inverse-form DFP trajectory satisfying strong Wolfe conditions while its gradient norms converge to a positive limit.

What is formalized

  • the two-phase DFP orbit and invariant slow curve;
  • the limiting circle and all-pairs separation estimate;
  • the global C2 uniformly convex realization;
  • all-dimensional transport and identity initialization.
